Data & method

Term premium and 10-year fitted yield: Adrian, Crump & Moench (ACM) daily series, Federal Reserve Bank of New York (ACMTP10, ACMY10), file ACMTermPremium.xls. S&P 500 daily close: Yahoo Finance (^GSPC). Series are aligned on common trading days.

Duration: modified duration of a 10-year par bond with semiannual coupons at yield y: Dmod = [1 − (1 + y/2)−20] / y. Window statistics: changes between the first and last trading day in the window; correlation is between daily changes in the term premium and daily S&P 500 log returns inside the window.
